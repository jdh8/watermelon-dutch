# Negative 1♦

This relay contains all hands with no other suitable call.

| Strength | Distribution |
|-------|---|
| 0--5  | Unwilling to play 1♣
| 6--11 | 0--3♠, 0--3♥, probably UNBAL
| 16+   | 2--3♠, 2--3♥, 3--4♦, 3--4♣

Dutch 1♣ is limited by the strong 2♣ opening and hardly has any 5-card major.
Unlike Polish 1♣, the strongest hands in the Dutch 1♣ are still quasi-natural,
either relatively balanced or long in clubs.

| 1♣ - 1♦ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♥♠  | 11--17, (4+# or BAL MIN 3=#)
| 1NT  | 18--20, 1--4♠, 1--4♥, 1--4♦, 2--5♣
| 2♣   | 11--17, 5+♣
| 2♦!  | 21--23, 1--4♠, 1--4♥, 1--4♦, 2--5♣
| 2♥♠  | 18--20, 4+#, 5+♣
| 2NT! | 18--20, 6+♣
| 3♣   | 15--17, 6+♣
