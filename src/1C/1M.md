I propose [445566-like][445566] step responses from Polish Club.  [Ingberman]
has a point to bring Lebensohl from 2NT down to a cheaper 4SF.  I take this idea
further.  Borrowing the step responses, it is now possible to play a 7-card fit
at the 2-level.
