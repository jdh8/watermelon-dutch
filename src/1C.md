# Dutch 1♣

My approach to respond to the 1♣ opening is a lawyer's Polish Club.  The 2♦
response is natural and game-forcing, while 1♦ is a generic catchall.  This way,
1♣ - 2♦ would pass more system regulations around the globe.

System regulations are more liberal in the Netherlands and most of Europe.
Dutch players often adopt conventional jump responses.  For example,
[Brink--Drijver] and [Rosalind Hengeveld][rosalind] play the Multi 2♦ response.

[Brink--Drijver]:https://www.bridge.nl/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Lb1_Brink-Drijver.pdf
[rosalind]: https://rosalind.home.xs4all.nl/bridge/2-over-1/index.htm

In countries with more conservative regulations, conventional jump responses are
restricted.  On the other hand, artificial 1♣ - 1♦ is often tolerated.  The
[ACBL Basic Chart][ACBL] and [🇯🇵 JCBL Convention List C][JCBL] explicitly
allow any 1♦ response to a constructive 1♣.

[ACBL]: https://web2.acbl.org/documentLibrary/about/Convention-Charts.pdf
[JCBL]: https://jcbl.sakura.ne.jp/handbook/HB2025p082-096%E3%82%B3%E3%83%B3%E3%83%99%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%B3%E3%83%AA%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88.pdf

## When to open on doubleton

I strongly recommend 5542 openings when 18+.  Dutch 1♣ is clearly a better
handler than 1♦ for strong balanced hands.

I'd even steal strong (43)42 from 1♦, but watch out for legal issues.  [ACBL]
considers 1♣ **quasi**-natural when it includes doubletons other than 4432. That
affects the right to bid artificial raises, e.g. 1♣ - 2NT = preemptive 6+♣.

I personally prefer 5533 on balanced minimum.  That is not a strong opinion.
